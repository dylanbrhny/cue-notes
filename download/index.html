Download Cue

Synapse’s call coach and note taker. Sign in with your work email and the team PIN to download.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11

Mac

Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

Installing on Windows

  1. Open Cue-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Cue is our own app, so it isn’t registered with Microsoft.
  3. Cue installs and opens by itself. Sign in with your @synapsemfg.com email and the team PIN.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Open Cue.dmg from your Downloads and drag Cue into Applications.
  2. Open Cue from Applications. If macOS says it can’t check it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Cue is our own app, so it isn’t registered with Apple.
  3. Allow the microphone and screen recording when asked (that’s how it hears calls), then sign in with your @synapsemfg.com email and the team PIN.

After this, Cue updates itself.
