How it works

One click when the call starts. Notes when it ends.

  1. Join your call as usual

    Open your Meet, Zoom or Teams call the way you always do. Cue asks if you want notes. Click Start.

  2. Talk

    Cue writes down both sides while you talk. Add your own notes in the notepad if you like.

  3. Hang up

    Cue writes the notes and a title in about a minute. They are waiting in the app the next time you look.

Questions

Questions people ask before they install it.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. Cue listens on your own computer, through your microphone and your speakers, so nothing joins the call. Because nothing announces it, let people know you are taking notes.

Which calls does it work with?

Cue starts on its own for Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack huddles, Cal Video and Webex. For anything else, click Start a call in Cue.

Why does my computer warn me about Cue?

Windows and macOS warn about any app that isn’t registered (“signed”) with Microsoft or Apple. Cue is built by Synapse for our own team and clients, so it isn’t registered yet. The warning is about the missing registration, not about anything Cue does.

Is it safe?

Yes. Cue only records the calls you start or allow, and keeps them in a private workspace. Clients only see their own calls. The download is checked against a fingerprint before it’s saved, so a damaged or swapped file is refused.

Who can use it?

Synapse’s team, and people at Synapse’s clients who have been invited. Clients only ever see their own calls. Their Synapse team sees them too, so you are never asked to send notes over.

I don’t have an invite. How do I get one?

Ask your Synapse contact. They add your email and send you a personal code to sign in with.

Do I go through the install warnings again for updates?

No. Cue updates itself. You only see the warnings the first time you install it on a computer.
