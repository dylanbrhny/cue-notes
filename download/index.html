Install it in a few minutes

1

Drag Cue into Applications

Open Cue.dmg from your Downloads. In the window that opens, drag Cue onto the Applications folder.

2

Open it once: your Mac will stop it

Open Cue from Applications. macOS says it can’t check Cue for malware, because Cue is our own app and isn’t registered with Apple yet. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3

Click Open Anyway in System Settings

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. Next to “Cue was blocked to protect your Mac”, click Open Anyway.

Confirm

Click Open Anyway again, then use Touch ID or your Mac password. You only do this once. After that Cue opens normally and updates itself.

Allow what it asks for

Cue asks for three permissions the first time. Click Allow on each: the microphone (to hear you), System Audio Recording Only (to hear the other side of the call; it never records your screen) and Accessibility (to spot when a Meet, Zoom or Teams call starts).
